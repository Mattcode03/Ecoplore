About this stay

Tucked into the pine-scented hillsides of Old Manali at 2,050 m, Mud House is a handcrafted retreat built entirely from rammed earth, stone, and reclaimed timber. The structure has stood for over a decade as a quiet testament that luxury and ecological responsibility are not mutually exclusive — here, thick mud walls keep rooms 6°C cooler in summer and naturally warm in October's chill.

Wake to unobstructed views of the Beas valley and Hanuman Tibba peak. Spend evenings by the cedar-wood firepit, sharing meals prepared from vegetables harvested that morning from the on-site kitchen garden. The owners, a family from Kullu, have lived here for three generations and pass on stories of the mountains that no guidebook can replicate.

With only four guest rooms, the property maintains an intentional intimacy. There is no pool, no gym, and no room service — only clean mountain air, deep quiet, and the kind of unhurried hospitality that reminds you why you left the city.

    Two 250-litre solar collectors installed 2021; LP gas backup used only October–March.

    Seasonal produce from a 0.2-acre kitchen garden; market-sourced items are local within 30 km.

    100% of construction work contracted to artisans from Kullu district; payroll records reviewed by Ecoplore.
